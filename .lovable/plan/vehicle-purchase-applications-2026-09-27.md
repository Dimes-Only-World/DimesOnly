# Vehicle purchase applications

## What will change
- Add **Purchase** as a vehicle product option in the Rentals admin area, alongside the existing rental choices.
- Show purchase actions only on vehicles marked for purchase, on both the Rentals collection and vehicle details.
- Add a professional buyer application with the uploaded buyer, optional co-buyer, residence, employment, interested vehicle, and trade-in fields.
- Use dropdowns for years from 2010 through the current year, makes, and matching models for interested and trade-in vehicles.
- Replace every customer-facing Stock Number field with VIN Number.
- Replace Barraza wording with **Best Car Rental Services**, using **Best** where a short label is needed.

## Privacy and submission
- Do not collect Social Security numbers in this online form; applicants can provide them later through the lender’s secure process.
- Validate required fields in the form and again when submitted.
- Save applications privately and make them available only to authorized rental admins.
- Add admin application statuses and a readable application review panel.

## Verification
- Test purchase eligibility, vehicle preselection, buyer and co-buyer paths, make/model dropdowns, trade-in fields, validation, submission, and admin review.
- Verify phone, tablet, and desktop layouts and confirm the app builds without errors.
